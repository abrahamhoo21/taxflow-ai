# INLAND REVENUE BOARD OF MALAYSIA (LEMBAGA HASIL DALAM NEGERI MALAYSIA - LHDN)
## e-INVOICE SPECIFIC GUIDELINES
**Publication Date:** June 2024  
**Scope:** Specific B2B, B2C, B2G, Cross-Border, and Self-Billed Transactions  

---

### [Page 4] Section 1: Business-to-Consumer (B2C) Transactions
#### 1.1 Individual Buyer Requirements
When issuing invoices to individual retail consumers who do not require an e-Invoice for personal income tax deduction purposes, suppliers may issue regular receipts or invoices as per existing practices.
However, if the buyer requests an e-Invoice:
- The supplier MUST issue an individual validated e-Invoice to the buyer.
- The buyer must provide: Full Name, NRIC/Passport Number, TIN (or General TIN: EI00000000020 if none), Address, Phone Number.

#### 1.3 Consolidated e-Invoice (Monthly Aggregation)
For consumers who do not request an individual e-Invoice:
- Suppliers are permitted to consolidate transactions into a single **Consolidated e-Invoice**.
- **Deadline**: The consolidated e-Invoice must be submitted to LHDN within **7 calendar days** following the end of the transaction month.
- Aggregation is NOT permitted for high-value items, luxury vehicles, or sales of goods to foreign tourists claiming Tourist Tax Refund.

### [Page 11] Section 2: Cross-Border & Foreign Supplier Transactions
#### 2.1 Importation of Goods and Services
When a Malaysian business imports services or goods from a foreign supplier outside Malaysia:
- The foreign supplier will not issue an LHDN e-Invoice.
- The Malaysian buyer is legally required to issue a **Self-Billed e-Invoice** to document the expenditure for income tax deductibility under Section 33(1) of the ITA 1967.
- **Timing of Self-Billed e-Invoice**: Must be issued upon payment made to the foreign supplier or receipt of foreign invoice, whichever is earlier.

#### 2.4 Exportation of Goods and Services
When exporting to overseas buyers:
- The Malaysian exporter must issue an e-Invoice with Buyer TIN recorded as `EI00000000030` (General foreign buyer TIN) and Buyer Registration Number as `NA`.
- The Custom Export Declaration (K2 Form) reference must be cross-referenced in the line item descriptions.

### [Page 18] Section 3: Disbursements vs Reimbursements
#### 3.1 Disbursements (Agent Capacity)
A disbursement occurs when an entity pays an amount on behalf of the client acting strictly as an agent:
- The supplier issues the invoice directly under the client's name.
- The agent does not include the payment in its own e-Invoice, as it does not constitute revenue or expense for the agent.

#### 3.2 Reimbursements (Principal Capacity)
A reimbursement occurs when an entity incurs expenses on its own account in the course of providing services to a client (e.g., flight, accommodation):
- The agent must issue an e-Invoice to the client including the reimbursed expenses as taxable line items.
- Any applicable Service Tax (SST) must be charged if the agent is a registered taxable person.
