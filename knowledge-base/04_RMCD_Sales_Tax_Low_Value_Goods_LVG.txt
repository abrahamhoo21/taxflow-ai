# ROYAL MALAYSIAN CUSTOMS DEPARTMENT (JABATAN KASTAM DIRAJA MALAYSIA - RMCD)
## GUIDE ON SALES TAX ON LOW VALUE GOODS (LVG)
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2024  
**Governing Legislation:** Sales Tax Act 2018 (Part III A - Section 11A to 11N)  

---

### [Page 1] Chapter 1: Legislative Background & Scope
#### 1.1 Purpose of Sales Tax on LVG
Prior to 2024, goods imported into Malaysia with a CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight) value under de minimis threshold (RM 500) were exempted from sales tax and import duty. To create a level playing field between local retailers and foreign online sellers, Malaysia introduced Sales Tax on Low Value Goods (LVG) effective **1 January 2024**.

#### 1.2 Definition of Low Value Goods (LVG)
Low Value Goods (LVG) are defined as:
- All goods (excluding cigarettes, tobacco products, intoxicating liquors, and smoking pipes)
- Sold at a price **not exceeding RM 500.00** per item/consignment
- Brought into Malaysia from outside Malaysia via air, sea, or land postal and courier services.

---

### [Page 5] Chapter 2: Tax Rate and Calculation
#### 2.1 Prescribed Sales Tax Rate
The prescribed rate of Sales Tax on LVG is **10% flat rate**.

#### 2.2 Tax Base Calculation
The 10% sales tax is charged strictly on the **sale price of the goods**, EXCLUDING:
- Transportation and delivery charges
- Insurance fees
- Any duties or import levies imposed on the delivery.

*Example Calculation:*
A consumer buys a mechanical keyboard from a foreign seller for RM 300, with RM 40 international shipping fee.
- Taxable Base = RM 300
- Sales Tax on LVG (10%) = RM 30.00
- Total amount payable by consumer = RM 300 + RM 30 (Tax) + RM 40 (Shipping) = **RM 370.00**.

---

### [Page 9] Chapter 3: Registration and Compliance
#### 3.1 Who Must Register as Registered Seller (RS)?
Both foreign and local sellers, including online marketplaces (such as Shopee, Lazada, Amazon, Taobao, or standalone e-commerce sites):
- If the total sales value of LVG sold to consumers in Malaysia exceeds **RM 500,000 within a 12-month period**.
- Registered sellers are assigned an **LVG Registration Number** which must be printed on the consignment package label to prevent double taxation at the Royal Malaysian Customs checkpoint.
